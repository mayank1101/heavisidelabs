Forta Health

Research Contributor & Co-Author
Clinical AI Research San Francisco, USA

Co-authored and contributed to OpenMedLM, an open-source prompting framework demonstrating that structured prompt-engineering strategies can outperform traditional parameter fine-tuning on complex medical question-answering benchmarks.

RESEARCH BREAKTHROUGH

SOTA Clinical Reasoning at Lower Compute

The architecture set new open-source standards by delivering enterprise-grade clinical reasoning performance with significantly lower compute overhead compared to weight fine-tuning.

  • Achieved 72.6% accuracy on MedQA (+2.4 points over the prior open-source state-of-the-art).
  • Became the first open-source model to surpass 80% accuracy on the MMLU medical subset.
  • Validated modular prompt strategies that eliminate expensive re-training cycles when foundation models update.
PEER REVIEW

AAAI 2024 Spring Symposium Publication

This research was peer-reviewed, accepted, and published in the proceedings of the AAAI 2024 Spring Symposium on Clinical Foundation Models.

  • OpenMedLM framework released as open source for the healthcare AI community.
  • Standardized benchmark evaluation harness for rigorous medical LLM verification.
