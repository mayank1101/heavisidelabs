The name

Oliver Heaviside was a self-taught engineer who rewrote Maxwell's equations into the compact form used today, and gave his name to the step function — the simplest possible way to describe a system flipping from one state to a clear, decided other.

That's the job we sign up for. Most organizations don't have a data problem so much as a decision problem: the data exists, but nobody can act on it with confidence. We build the models, pipelines, and dashboards that make the step from "we think" to "we know."

How we work

A signal chain, not a black box.

Four stages, run in order, each one handed to you in a state you can inspect.

01 — CAPTURE

Understand the data you have

We audit sources, quality, and gaps before proposing anything — no architecture decided in a vacuum.

02 — MODEL

Build the smallest thing that works

Pipelines, models, or dashboards, scoped to the decision they need to support — not the trend of the year.

03 — SHIP

Put it in front of real users

Deployed into your stack, documented, and handed off with your team able to operate it independently.

04 — MONITOR

Watch it, then tighten it

We instrument what we ship, so drift and edge cases surface early instead of at the worst moment.
